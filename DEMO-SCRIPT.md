# GhostBird Glance — Demo Video Script (target 2:30, hard limit 3:00)

Record the **simulator** (`evenhub-simulator http://localhost:5173`) or the
**browser preview** (http://localhost:5173 in any browser — same screens,
click rows to select, click = TAP, double-click = DOUBLE-TAP on text
screens). Screen-record at 1080p; the G2 canvas is small, so zoom the
simulator window / browser to fill the frame. Speak the lines or use
captions — either is fine.

Before recording: open the app once, let prices load, and place NO trades
(balance should read $1,000.00, 0 open positions). If a previous session
left positions, clear site storage / bridge storage for a clean start.

---

**[0:00–0:18] The problem**
*On screen: home glance.*
> "Crypto holders check prices and odds dozens of times a day — and every
> check means fishing out a phone. GhostBird Glance puts both on the Even
> G2 lens, and lets you act on them with a tap."

**[0:18–0:42] The glance (Hyperliquid)**
*On screen: home. Wait for the 30s tick or tap "Refresh prices".*
> "The home screen is a live glance: Bitcoin, Ether and Solana prices
> with 24-hour change, streamed from Hyperliquid's public API and
> refreshed every thirty seconds. Below it, my paper portfolio at a
> glance — balance, open positions, live profit and loss."

**[0:42–1:05] The markets (Polymarket)**
*Select "Prediction markets". Scroll the list slowly.*
> "One tap opens Polymarket's live Bitcoin prediction markets, ranked by
> 24-hour volume, each with its live YES odds. These are real markets,
> updating in real time — already-resolved markets are filtered out,
> because there's no fun paper-trading a done deal."

**[1:05–1:35] Paper trade — the write path**
*Select a market near 50–60% YES. Show the detail screen. Select
"Buy YES - $25". Hold on the confirm screen. TAP to confirm.*
> "Here's a market. I can paper-trade it right from the glasses — YES or
> NO, twenty-five or a hundred paper dollars. Nothing executes on one
> tap: the confirm screen shows shares, price and balance after, TAP
> confirms, DOUBLE-TAP cancels. Filled — forty-odd shares at the live
> price, from a virtual thousand-dollar balance. No wallet, no keys, no
> real funds — that's deliberate."

**[1:35–2:02] Positions, marked to market**
*On the filled screen, TAP to view positions. Open the position.*
> "Positions are marked to market against live Polymarket odds, so the
> profit and loss on this screen is real math on real prices. I can
> close at market from the glasses the same way I opened — one more
> confirm screen, and the PnL is realized into the balance. Everything
> persists on-device between sessions."

**[2:02–2:22] Safety + offline**
*Back home.*
> "Safety is the design: read-only public APIs, paper money only, no
> custody, no financial advice. And if the network drops, the app falls
> back to a labelled snapshot instead of a dead screen."

**[2:22–2:30] Close**
> "GhostBird Glance — prices and odds at a glance, action in two taps.
> Built solo for the Crypto and DeFi track. Thank you."
